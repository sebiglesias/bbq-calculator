Frequently Asked Questions

Why does brisket lose so much weight during smoking?

A full packer beef brisket typically undergoes a 40% to 50% total weight reduction. Approximately 15% is lost during the initial trimming of hard deckle fat and thin silver skin. The remaining 30% to 35% is moisture evaporation and intramuscular fat rendering during the 12 to 16-hour low-and-slow smoke at 225°F–250°F.

How many racks of ribs for 10 adults?

For standard St. Louis cut pork ribs (12 to 13 bones per slab), plan on **3 to 4 racks for 10 adults** (approx 3 to 4 ribs per person when served alongside other meats like pulled pork or brisket).
